# Hooks and Holders
Things for hanging or holding in place other things. I began this section because I wanted to hang my headphones and skateboard securely. These are simple blocky hooks that can of course be used for other things. 

My favourite technique to adhere these to things is using a soldering iron to push heated metal into them. In the case of the single hook, the structure I adhered too already had two metal prongs for placement. I heated these up and melted the single hooks onto them. In the case of the dual hook, which came after, I added two small holes to guide a nail through the hook which could then be hammered or screwed into the wall. A second version of the single hook will have a hole or pocket added to guide a metal insert.
